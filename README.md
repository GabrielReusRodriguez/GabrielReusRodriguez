## About me

Software engineer with **18+ years** of experience building and optimizing systems in real-world environments: healthcare, public administration, insurance, and academia.

Since 2014 I work as an **analyst at Consorci Sanitari Integral**, where I develop clinical integrations, WebServices, and solutions around SAP IS-H, HL7, Java, Python, and XSL. I am especially interested in **healthcare interoperability**, **local AI infrastructure**, and contributing to **open source** projects.
